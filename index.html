What counts as a revision?

One revision round is one combined list of reasonable adjustments after you review a draft, such as text, colors, spacing or image changes. Corrections to defects against our agreed specification do not use up revision rounds. Once your included rounds are used, further revisions are available at a separately negotiated price based on the size and complexity of the requested changes. New pages, major redesigns and new features also require a new quote, always agreed before work begins.

Flexible, transparent prices

All listed prices are starting estimates in USD (US dollars), negotiable before ordering where permitted by the platform. Domain, hosting, paid third-party tools, content writing and ongoing maintenance are separate unless explicitly agreed.
